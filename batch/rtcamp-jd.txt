Application Process

Job hunting can be a challenging endeavor, so we try not to add any mystery. Guaranteed initial screening & response within 5 working days.

Screening

We check your application for completeness, missing info and run it through a review checklist.

Interview

You are invited to a Zoom call with an interview panel at a time that is convenient for you.

You’re Hired! 🎉

We send you a formal offer. This usually arrives within 2-3 days of your interview.

Working at rtCamp
Work From Anywhere

We’re a 100% distributed company that works asynchronously, with a team spanning 13 countries and counting.

Flexible Hours

You can pick your own schedule according to when you’re most productive, with the flexibility to do split-scheduling.

Paid Leaves

We offer a minimum of 21 days of paid time off to support rest and work-life balance. In addition, 4 national holidays are provided based on the engagement model.

Open Source

You’ll have the opportunity to add to rtCampers’ unbroken streak of 34 contributions to WordPress Core.

You can count on the process being transparent, prompt & straightforward. No application black holes here! 🔭
